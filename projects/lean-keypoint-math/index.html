What is proved

  • 1 · Scale space The per-layer blurs stack to exactly σ₀kⁱ. Each octave starts at σ₀ again.
  • 2 · DoG ≈ σ²∇²G The DoG over k − 1 tends to σ²∇²G. A bright point is a DoG minimum.
  • 3 · Extrema The 26-neighbour test is non-strict and checks the sign.
  • 4–5 · Sub-pixel fit and contrast The offset is the model's only stationary point. The contrast is its value there.
  • 6 · Edge test A point stays when both curvatures have the same sign and differ by less than 10×.
  • 7 · Orientation peak The parabola offset is the peak, within half a bin.
  • 8 · Descriptor bins OpenCV's splits are trilinear weights. They sum to 1.
  • 9 · Normalization One clip at 0.2‖v‖ equals Lowe's normalize, clip, normalize.
  • Constants The default contrast threshold is 1/75, under half of Lowe's 0.03.

Each file lists what it does not model. SURF, ORB, BRISK and AKAZE are not started.
